The BMW M 1000 RR is a high-performance track-focused superbike built with race-derived aerodynamics and a powerful ShiftCam engine.

Features of BIKE

Key Engine and Performance Features

Uses BMW ShiftCam technology, titanium valves, and a lightweight exhaust system to generate maximum power at high RPMs. Produces up to 212–215 hp at 14,500 rpm and 113 Nm of torque at 11,000 rpm.

Aerodynamics and Bodywork

Generates significant aerodynamic downforce to keep the front wheel planted during high-speed acceleration. The aerodynamic design improves stability during aggressive track riding.

Electronics and Rider Aids

Includes multiple selectable modes such as Rain, Road, Dynamic and Race alongside advanced rider assistance and telemetry.

Specification

  • Displacement: 999 cc
  • Max Power: 212.10 PS
  • Max Torque: 113 Nm
  • Transmission: 6-speed gearbox
  • Top Speed: ~306 km/h
  • Drive: Chain Drive
  • Seating: 2
  • Fuel: Petrol
  • Body Type: Sports Bike
