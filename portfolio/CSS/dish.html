Paneer Tikka

Paneer tikka is a popular Indian dish made from chunks of paneer marinated in spices and grilled in a tandoor. It is a vegetarian alternative to chicken tikka and is often served hot with mint chutney. The dish typically includes paneer, bell peppers, onions, and sometimes tomatoes, all marinated in a spiced yogurt mixture before being grilled.

Ingridents

    Paneer – 250 g, cubes
  • Capsicum – 1, cubes
  • Onion – 1, cubes
  • Curd (Dahi) – ½ cup
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste – 1 tsp
  • Red Chilli Powder – 1 tsp
  • Turmeric Powder (Haldi) – ½ tsp
  • Garam Masala – ½ tsp
  • Cumin Powder (Jeera) – ½ tsp
  • Chaat Masala – ½ tsp
  • Lemon Juice – 1 tbsp
  • Salt – as per taste
  • Oil – 1 tbsp
  • Kasuri Methi – ½ tsp
  • Coriander Leaves – for garnishing
